Apple uses the opaque RGB 1024-pixel master at assets/icon-only.png to generate both iPhone and iPad icons in the existing build. Android includes legacy, round and adaptive resources for all five densities, plus the master. Adaptive artwork is centered at 58dp within the 108dp canvas; important artwork fits within the 66dp safe circle. Preview checks cover circle, square and rounded masks and small sizes. Small lettering is inherently limited at launcher sizes; native installed-icon inspection is still required before claiming device verification.
