# AndroidX Media3 control artwork

The native iOS transport artwork in `scripts/IrgunUnifiedPlayerPlugin.swift`
adapts AndroidX Media3's `exo_ic_rewind`, `exo_ic_forward`,
`exo_ic_play_circle_filled`, and `exo_ic_pause_circle_filled` vector paths.
The paths were translated to UIKit drawing commands to match the Android app.
